# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in TapeDB to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds a healthy project:

- Being direct about technical problems without attacking the person.
- Giving and accepting specific, evidence-based feedback.
- Respecting different experience levels and operational constraints.
- Crediting work and acknowledging uncertainty.
- Keeping reports and reproductions free of secrets and personal data.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination or sexualized attention.
- Insults, personal attacks or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, customer data or another person's claim tokens.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and any
other project space, and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, reject contributions or
restrict participation when behaviour violates this policy. Enforcement should
be proportionate, documented privately and focused on stopping the harm.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through
GitHub. Do not use the public issue tracker. Reports will be reviewed as
confidentially as the platform allows.
